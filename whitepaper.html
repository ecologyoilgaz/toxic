01. Abstract

The ECO Impact Token (€ECO) is an institutional-grade, RWA-backed ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) asset deployed on the Solana blockchain. Built on the advanced Token-2022 standard, €ECO introduces a native on-chain "Green Audit" mechanism. By leveraging Transfer Hooks, every transaction is tracked for its carbon footprint, ensuring full compliance with modern ESG standards.

Unlike traditional "green" tokens that rely on promises, €ECO is backed by a physical vault containing responsibly mined gold and emerald reserves, providing a hard price floor and tangible value.

03. Technology: Token-2022 & Green Audit

Standard SPL tokens on Solana are static. €ECO utilizes Token-2022 with the Transfer Hook extension. This means we have a smart contract that intercepts every transfer.

Why is this important? It allows us to programmatically calculate the carbon footprint of every transaction and automatically route a percentage of network fees to verified carbon offset registries (like Verra). This creates a self-sustaining, transparent ESG ecosystem.

04. The RWA Eco-Vault

Trust requires real-world backing. The Eco-Vault holds:

  • Responsibly Mined Gold: Certified by the Fairmined standard.
  • Emerald Reserves: High-quality, conflict-free gemstones.

These assets are audited quarterly. In the event of a severe market downturn, the vault implements a 20% Buyback Discount, using the physical assets to buy back €ECO from the open market and burn it, creating a hard price floor.
